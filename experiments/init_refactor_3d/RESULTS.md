# Insights and conclusions

*Sweep complete (336 / 336 cells + the 42-cell `c1e4_exp1.0` extension,
2026-08-25). Regenerate the tables with `python experiments/report.py
init_refactor_3d`; this section is preserved. (The "runs collected" header counts
c1e4 against all four step counts, hence 378 / 504 — every cell that exists is in.)*

- **The general-d ELBO holds at d = 3.** Bayes-optimal model, 32M samples:
  `wnelbo_ref = 0.50067 ± 0.00077` on naive_ps (H = 0.50029); φ-independent to
  ±0.0015 for embeddings with rare words packed within 0.003 rad (the bound only
  degrades for *exactly* coincident words, as it must).
- **Training at d = 3 is easy and rate-insensitive.** At 20k steps every CE cell
  is within ~0.002 of H on naive_ps, including the above-cliff rates exp0.5–1.0
  that at d = 2 sit 0.01–0.03 high. PP-trained cells behave like d = 2: slightly
  above H, converging with steps, noisier.
- **`wce_ref` is ~4× smaller than at d = 2** (0.54 vs 2.23 for CE-trained
  naive_ps): the `(d-1)/2` drift identifies the word earlier in `t`.
- **Read the seed-0 100k/200k CE rows with care: they sit 0.003–0.006 *below* H on
  both datasets and all rates. This is not a leak.** A re-trained copy of the
  exp0.1/200k cell reproduced the pipeline reading (0.4967) but, re-evaluated on
  three fresh 8M-sample draws, read 0.5015 / 0.5022 / 0.4998, and on identical
  samples it scores a stable +0.0004 *above* the Bayes posterior on every draw
  (+0.00041 / +0.00039 / +0.00044) — so on the pipeline's draw the Bayes model
  itself would read ≈ 0.4963. The cause is that every seed-0 cell with the same
  `max_steps` — both datasets, all 14 (rate, geometry) cells — sees the *same test
  sample*: the reference-path `t` comes from `_make_step_generator` (seed ×
  global_step × batch_idx × stage, nothing run-specific), and the bridge noise and
  shuffled labels come from the global RNG, whose consumption during training
  depends only on `max_steps`. One ~-1.5 SE realisation of the heavy-tailed
  estimator therefore moves the whole group. (Replaying only the `t` part for the
  Bayes model gives -1.0 / -1.4 / -1.3 / +0.3 SE at 20k / 40k / 100k / 200k on
  naive_ps — correlated, but the bridge-noise part matters as much.) At d = 9 / 16
  the effect is 5–10× larger (estimator std 23–45 vs 4.3), which is why whole
  step-groups there jump by ±0.05. Seeds 1–2 decorrelate it; for cross-cell
  comparisons at fixed seed, re-evaluate the saved `model.pt` on fresh draws or
  give the test pass its own seed.
- **V = 10^4 (`c1e4_exp1.0`, H = 1.0407, 20k steps, 3 seeds):** CE-trained cells
  are on H at every loss rate (1.0435–1.0470, vs 1.05–1.25 at d = 2 where the low
  rates wander), `wce_ref` 0.93 vs 4.3–6.5 at d = 2. PP-trained cells improve
  4–35× over d = 2 (1.054–1.111 vs 1.45–3.77) but at 20k steps still trail CE by
  0.01–0.07 with `wce_ref` ≈ 40–60. The extra boundary dimension separates 10^4
  words far better than the circle does.
