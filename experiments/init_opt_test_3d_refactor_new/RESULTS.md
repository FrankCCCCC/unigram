# Insights and conclusions

**All 504 cells completed; 0 failures.** `mode=opt` means every number here is the
*exact* posterior's, so nothing below is a statement about a model — it is a statement
about the importance-weighted estimator at each curvature.

## 1. The reference pass is exactly invariant, as designed

`wnelbo_ref`, `nelbo_ref`, `wce_ref` and `ce_ref` are byte-identical across all 14
(loss_geometry × rate) cells of every `(ps, K, seed)` group — 36 groups, max spread
**2.2e-16 (one ulp)**. Only `wloss`, the swept objective, varies. The `salt=0` / `salt=1`
RNG split in `BaseTrainer._make_step_generator` does what it claims: the reference metrics
are comparable across cells trained on different objectives and different proposal rates.

## 2. Curvature decides whether the ELBO is measurable at all

Averaged over all 168 cells per `(ps, K)`; `SE = std / sqrt(4e6)`:

| ps | K | `wnelbo_ref` | H(p) | (x−H)/SE | std | `wce_ref` | `ce_ref` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| naive_ps | **−0.01** | **0.3594** | 0.5003 | **−3.3** | 85.4 | 44.4 | 0.465 |
| naive_ps | −1.0 | 0.5013 | 0.5003 | +0.5 | 4.3 | 0.63 | 0.056 |
| naive_ps | −10.0 | 0.4974 | 0.5003 | −0.4 | 13.0 | 0.062 | 0.0062 |
| cmplx_ps | **−0.01** | **1.2439** | 1.6664 | **−3.8** | 220.0 | 172.0 | 1.556 |
| cmplx_ps | −1.0 | 1.6692 | 1.6664 | +0.9 | 6.5 | 2.10 | 0.186 |
| cmplx_ps | −10.0 | 1.6605 | 1.6664 | −0.6 | 19.7 | 0.209 | 0.021 |
| c1e3 / c1e4 | **−0.01** | **0.7667** | 1.0407 | **−5.2** | 105.0 | 90.3 | 0.946 |
| c1e3 / c1e4 | −1.0 | 1.0409 | 1.0407 | +0.1 | 5.4 | 1.15 | 0.103 |
| c1e3 / c1e4 | −10.0 | 1.0395 | 1.0407 | −0.1 | 16.3 | 0.115 | 0.011 |

**`K = -1` and `K = -10` are sound.** Every reading lands on `H(p)` within ±0.9 SE. The
`K = -1` slice also reproduces `init_refactor_3d` (`main.py`, same `d = 3`), so the
refactored horosphere-readout path did not move the number.

**`K = -0.01` is not.** The exact posterior reports **26 ± 2 % below `H(p)`** —
0.3594/0.5003, 1.2439/1.6664, 0.7667/1.0407 = 0.718 / 0.746 / 0.737. Quote the *relative*
deficit, not the SE ratio: the sample std is itself unstable here (85–220 against 4–6 at
`K = -1`), so `(x−H)/SE` swings between −3 and −16 depending on how much of the tail a
given subset caught. The deficit is stable; its error bar is not.

Since the model is exact by construction, `wnelbo_ref < H(p)` at `K = -0.01` **cannot** be
a side-channel leak. Invariant 2 has a precondition, and this is it: the invariant tests
the model only where the estimator can resolve the integral.

## 3. The mechanism: the integral moves, the proposal does not

`K` fixes `R = 1/sqrt(-K)`, and the path-KL integrand is a *rate* carrying `kappa^2 = -K`.
Going from `K = -1` to `K = -0.01` therefore does two opposite things at once:

- the integrand shrinks ~100× — visible in `nelbo_ref`, 0.0035 at `K = -0.01` vs 0.0445 at
  `K = -1`;
- the range it must be integrated over grows ~100×, since `main_refactor.py`'s heat-time
  ceiling is `_radial_t_max(3) · R^2` = 9700 vs 97.

The product is conserved — that is why `H(p)` is still the right answer — but the pinned
`exp(0.1)` reference draws `t` with mean 10 and weights it by `e^{0.1 t}/0.1`. The bulk of
a 9700-long integral is never sampled, and the estimator returns roughly the fraction it
did see. Equivalently: the ~0.304 variance cliff measured at `K = -1` scales as `1/R^2`,
so at `K = -0.01` it sits at ~0.00304 and the pinned reference rate is **32× above it**.

`ce_ref` corroborates the same picture from the other side: 0.47 / 1.56 / 0.95 nats at
`K = -0.01` against 0.056 / 0.186 / 0.103 at `K = -1` — at the `t` the proposal actually
draws, the flat-curvature bridge has barely concentrated and the word is still ambiguous.

## 4. `c1e3` and `c1e4` agree to all printed digits

At every curvature, the two geometric-law specs return identical `wnelbo_ref`, `wce_ref`,
`nelbo_ref` and `ce_ref`. This reproduces the known configuration-independence of the
Bayes-optimal Girsanov integrand documented in `init_refactor_3d/EXPERIMENT.md`. As there:
**they are one validation, not two independent ones** — do not quote them as mutual
corroboration.

## 5. What this licenses the trained projects to claim

- At `K = -1` and `K = -10`, `test_wnelbo_ref >= H(p)` is a valid test of the trained
  model, and the trained project's distance from `H(p)` is the model's own.
- At `K = -0.01`, it is not. A trained cell there must be compared to **0.3594 / 1.2439 /
  0.7667**, not to `H(p)`; reading it against `H(p)` would report a leak that is not there.
- Measuring the ELBO at a flat curvature needs the reference proposal re-tuned to that
  curvature (rate ~`0.1 · R^{-2}`), not the pinned `exp(0.1)`. That is a change to
  `setup.md`'s design, so it is flagged here rather than made unilaterally.
